WoW Reader

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how WoW Reader accesses, uses, stores and protects information when you use the Android app.

Effective date: 2 September 2026

1. Local-first operation

WoW Reader is designed to work offline. Books you import, reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, notes, reader preferences and custom fonts are stored locally on your device unless you choose to use the optional Google Account backup feature.

2. Google Account information

If you choose to connect a Google Account, WoW Reader uses Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication to authenticate you. The app may receive basic account information provided by Google, such as your account identifier, display name, email address and profile photo. This information is used only to identify the signed-in account and show the connected account inside the app.

3. Google Drive app-data access

For cloud backup and restore, WoW Reader requests access to Google Drive's application data folder (drive.appdata / appDataFolder). This permission allows the app to create, update, read and delete files that WoW Reader itself stores in its private app-data space.

WoW Reader does not request permission to browse, read or modify your general Google Drive files. The app-data folder is used only for WoW Reader backup and restore.

4. Data included in a backup

When Google Drive backup is enabled, a WoW Reader backup may include:

  • EPUB and PDF files that you imported into WoW Reader;
  • reading progress and resume positions;
  • bookmarks, highlights and notes;
  • reader and library preferences;
  • custom reader fonts stored by the app.

The backup is stored in the connected Google Account's Google Drive app-data space so that you can restore the data on another device.

5. How Google user data is used

Google user data is used only to provide account sign-in, private backup, restore and synchronization features requested by the user. WoW Reader does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers.

6. Data sharing

WoW Reader does not intentionally sell or rent personal information. Information is transmitted to Google only as necessary to provide Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication and Google Drive app-data backup/restore. Third-party services are governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

7. Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you remove books, clear app data or uninstall the app. Google Drive backup data remains in the connected account's app-data space until it is replaced or deleted through the app or the user's Google Account controls. Signing out stops the app from using the connected account until you sign in again.

8. Security

WoW Reader uses Google-provided authentication and authorization mechanisms for Google Account access. No system can guarantee absolute security, but the app limits Google Drive access to the app-data scope needed for its backup feature.

9. Children's privacy

WoW Reader is a general-purpose reading application and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children beyond information provided through an optional Google Account connection.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when WoW Reader features or data practices change. The current version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

11. Contact

For privacy or support questions, visit saroatsin.com or contact the Whisper Of Words community through Telegram.

WoW Reader

A local-first EPUB and PDF reader for Android. Read offline, keep your notes and progress on-device, and optionally connect your Google Account to privately back up your library data to the app's Google Drive app-data space.

Android package: com.whisper.wowreader

What WoW Reader does

Offline readingImport EPUB and PDF books and read them without an internet connection.
Reading dataKeep reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, notes, reader settings and custom fonts.
Private cloud backupOptionally back up and restore app data using Google Drive's appDataFolder, which is separate from your normal Drive files.
Google Account access is optional.
WoW Reader uses Google Sign-In for account identity and requests only the Google Drive app-data permission needed for backup and restore. It does not request access to browse your general Google Drive files.
